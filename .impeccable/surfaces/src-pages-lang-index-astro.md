## Direction contract

THESIS: The home is one continuous flight through a single living WebGL galaxy. A lit route threads every system; each featured project is a star system the camera arrives at, and the page ends by pulling back to show the whole body of work as constellations. It refuses pinned slogan screens and the horizontal card strip.

OWN-WORLD: The existing Cosmic Keynote world, now in real depth: deep space #03030a, nebula blue #2997ff / violet #a259ff / pink #ff5e8a / cyan #64d2ff, each planet tinted by its project's cardA→cardB pair; Inter 860 display at -0.04em, Space Grotesk labels, Al Rai Media Arabic display; glass only where it holds a real screenshot; theme packs re-tint the galaxy.

STORY: One line says what he does → five beats (map, trace, connect, guard, prove) while the camera dives in → eight featured systems, the four public repositories first with live links and honest boundaries → the Sky Map: every project, filterable, each a star → contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The galaxy seen from outside at a low three-quarter angle, core glow slightly above centre; centred two-line H1 "I turn manual work. / Into systems that run." at display scale, 46ch lead beneath, primary CTA "See the systems" and secondary "Get in touch"; a faint lit route already visible threading the arms; scroll cue at the bottom edge.

FORM: One Sky flight — option 1 of 3 put to the owner through the structured question round (Constellation index and Depth cards declined). No concept-seed key: direction chosen in the owner's own words.

Signature interaction: scroll drives the camera along the route (damped, arrival-weighted); station screenshots sit in three CSS depth layers that parallax against the moving planet; Sky Map card hover/focus lights its star and floats a label in 3D.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Mohamed Mahmoud for Automation Engineer, AI automation, systems-integration, and internal-tools roles in Kuwait, the GCC, or remote teams. They need to understand what he built, what he personally owned, what was measured, and where each system’s limits are.

## Product Purpose

This bilingual English/Arabic portfolio presents Mohamed as an Automation Engineer through inspectable project stories. Success means a recruiter can quickly see the breadth of the work, open a story, understand the real operating problem and architecture, and trust that every number and status claim was sourced from the project repository.

## Positioning

Mohamed maps manual work, builds the local or internal system that replaces it, adds human approval and evidence boundaries, and verifies the result against real operating data. The portfolio demonstrates that mechanism across business automation, document production, desktop software, local AI, filesystem tooling, and simulation architecture.

## Operating Context

The work spans bilingual Kuwait business operations, offline and LAN-only internal tools, local AI, Windows/macOS desktop applications, print-production documents, measured system utilities, and engineering studies. Recruiters browse on phone and desktop and may enter through either the English or Arabic edition.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Astro 5 static site, deployed at the existing Netlify domain.
- Separate, indexable `/en/` and `/ar/` editions with mirrored story routes.
- Thirteen primary Automation Engineer stories, nine preserved foundation stories, and eight secondary Engineering Lab studies.
- Private repositories may inform verified copy but must never expose private URLs, credentials, personal identifiers, code, customer data, or third-party assets.
- Public repository or demo links appear only when the linked material is intentionally public.
- Platform status, shipping status, measurements, and limitations must use the exact scope documented by the corresponding repository.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the recovered live portfolio’s cinematic cosmic design language as the default Astronomy world, including its pinned story structure, luminous depth, bilingual behavior, motion system, and per-story art direction. Seven opt-in identity studies may reinterpret typography, material, atmosphere, transitions, and motion, but must never fork facts, routes, or story markup. Interface labels use a readable proportional face; monospace is reserved for genuine code or telemetry.

## Evidence on Hand

- The recovered nine-story portfolio and its Astro source in `flagship-portfolio`.
- README files from all repositories that make up the thirteen main and eight Lab project families.
- Public demos for Cake Studio and the portfolio design study, plus the public Polyblast Arena source.
- Repository-documented test counts, measurements, architecture notes, status boundaries, and known limitations.
- No permission to fabricate testimonials, commercial outcomes, certifications, deployment status, or private source links.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence before emphasis: a thinner true claim is stronger than an inflated one.
2. Show the operating mechanism: each story explains inputs, controls, outputs, proof, and limits.
3. Human authority stays visible: approval, rollback, audit, and fail-closed behavior are product features.
4. One truth in two languages: English and Arabic must carry the same facts and status.
5. Private work can demonstrate judgment without exposing private implementation or rights-restricted assets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic navigation, visible keyboard focus, correct RTL layout, readable contrast, mobile/desktop parity, and a reduced-motion path for all newly added surfaces.

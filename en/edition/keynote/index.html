How the film was made.

One HTML page, 1440 by 1440, at 120 beats a minute. Nothing fades, blurs or cuts: things transform, and a cursor makes every change happen.

  1. An iris, on the beatSix blades close over the label and snap open onto a photo.
  2. Liquid glass, computedEach glass shape bends a copy of the scene behind it, edge by edge.
  3. Phone to MacThe wallpaper is dragged onto a Safari tab and becomes the page.
  4. The real wallIt ends on a framed print, in footage of a wall with moving shadows.

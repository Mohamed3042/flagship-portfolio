Privacy

This notice covers the studio’s services, terms and contact pages. Last updated 7 October 2026.

Browsing the store

Our store has no account registration, payment form or analytics tracker. Its code does not set cookies. Your browser requests a country code from ipapi.co to choose a price list. The provider receives your IP address and handles it under its privacy policy. This store keeps only the selected market and redirect flag in this tab’s sessionStorage. If the lookup fails, the page, time zone and browser language provide a fallback. A prepared quote link can override the choice.

Hosting and other websites

GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to provide and protect its service. WhatsApp, email and LinkedIn open only when you follow their links and have their own privacy policies. The linked cinematic portfolio is a separate experience.

When you contact us

We use the information you send to respond, prepare your quote and deliver agreed work. Share only what we need for your enquiry. Do not send passwords or payment credentials. Project records are kept as needed for delivery, support and applicable record-keeping duties.

Your requests

Contact us by email to ask about information you shared or request a correction or deletion. We will explain if a delivery, legal or accounting obligation requires us to retain a record.

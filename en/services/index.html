Revisions & refunds

Before we start.

What do we need to get started?

Tell us about your business and the result you want. After you approve the quote, we send a short checklist for your logo, text, images and other material. Delivery time starts when the deposit and the agreed material are received.

Who owns the finished work?

You receive a licence to use the delivered work for your business. Our underlying software and reusable components remain ours. Your quote sets out exactly what is included.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds in every package. Extra rounds or work outside the agreed scope are quoted before we start them.

Arabic, English or both?

Either, or both. Arabic pages are designed from right to left from the start. Tell us what you need and your quote will cover it.

Will our project stay confidential?

Yes. We can sign an NDA before you share anything. Unreleased work is never shown, and completed work enters our portfolio only with your permission.

How does payment work?

50% to start and 50% on delivery. We confirm payment details with your written quote. Final files are released once the balance is paid.

What if plans change?

Before work starts, your deposit is refunded in full. After work starts, the deposit covers work already completed. If we miss the agreed date by more than 7 days, you may cancel and recover the part of the deposit for work not delivered.

Why do prices change by country?

Prices follow the country where your business operates and how you pay, not the number you message from. Egyptian prices are paid by Egyptian methods, from inside Egypt.

Tell us what you need.

An idea, a problem, or a business ready for its next step. We reply within 24 hours.

Questions

Before we start.

Who owns what you make?

You get a licence to use everything delivered to you (the film, the site or the system) for your business, for as long as you use it. The engine, source code and methods behind it stay mine: that is what keeps the work fast and the price fair.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds in every package. Extra rounds, or anything outside the agreed scope, are quoted before any work on them starts.

How long does it take?

Each package above shows its delivery time, and your plan gives the exact date. Once you say yes, you get a short checklist of what I need from you; the clock starts when the deposit and everything on it are in.

Arabic, English or both?

Either, or both. Arabic is my first language and my English is fluent. Arabic layouts are designed right to left from the start, not flipped at the end. Tell me what you need, and your quote covers it.

Will my project stay confidential?

Yes. I can sign an NDA before you share anything. Unreleased work is never shown, and finished work goes into my portfolio only with your OK.

How does payment work?

50% to start and 50% on delivery. Payment details come with your quote.

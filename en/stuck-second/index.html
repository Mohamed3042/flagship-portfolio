The Stuck Second: a film drawn in real time that you move through by scrolling. A broken pocket watch lies shut in the rain, stuck on the same second for years. Scrolling takes you under its engraved lid, across the cracked dial and through a hanging raindrop into the opening, then down into the movement: the frozen oil, the wheels straining against each other, the hairspring and the balance, and the one red jewel where the tooth is jammed. At the end the lid falls shut again on the first picture. Stop scrolling anywhere and the watch moves for you.

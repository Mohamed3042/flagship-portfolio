The channel

Al‑Ma’ali Satellite Channel

An Islamic broadcast network. For ~4 years I owned its social presence end-to-end — strategy, calendar, and per-platform optimization — turning broadcast clips into millions of views a month.

What I owned

The whole social function.

01

Cross-platform strategy

One content engine adapted to Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube — each with its own format and rhythm.

02

Calendar & consistency

A publishing calendar that kept output steady for years, not bursts.

03

Per-platform optimization

Hooks, lengths, captions and posting times tuned to what each platform rewards.

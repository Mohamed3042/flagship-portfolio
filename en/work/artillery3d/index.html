Architecture evidence

Scale is represented in data, not a shipped-game claim.

The repository documents 503 battle maps, 2,058 decoded crater masks and 199 recovered spawn lists, while also recording fallbacks and deviations.

Rights and status boundary

Presented as architecture and educational reverse-engineering work, not as an original shipped game or ownership of the referenced legacy content. Approximations and deviations stay labelled.

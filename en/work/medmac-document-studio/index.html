The document pipeline

Source to press-ready, with gates.

Generation and promotion are separate so a draft cannot silently become a final.

01

Ingest sources

PDF, DOCX, spreadsheets and text formats are hashed and extracted.

02

Normalize once

English and Arabic live in one strict content structure.

03

Render by tokens

Measured references become grids, rhythm, typography and bleed rules.

04

Verify the PDF

Boxes, fonts, ICC, page geometry and PDF/X-4 conformance are checked before promotion.

Production proof

Five finished documents, measured end to end.

The shipped set contains three 24-page documents and two 22-page documents, all produced through the same pipeline.

Evidence boundary

Source files are evidence, not permission to invent claims. Unverified certifications, exclusivity, statistics, customer data and AI imagery are excluded by design.

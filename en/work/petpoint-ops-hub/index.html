Renaming rules “AI” is not an improvement.

Measured on synthetic data

The leaderboard keeps its losers.

At the 7-day horizon a PyTorch GRU leads at 1.536 horizon-total MAE; at 14 days a trailing-mean baseline wins. On a panel with 76.2% zero days, forecasting nothing wins daily MASE while producing the worst forecast, so it stays in the table.

Honest boundary

Independent technical demo: not an official Pet Point product, not endorsed by Pet Point and not connected to its systems. Every record is synthetic, and the numbers measure this pipeline on that corpus — the method, not a business outcome. The 14-day grooming holdout holds 37 bookings and no no-shows, reported as not measurable rather than zero. AI-assisted build; Mohamed specified, reviewed and verified it.

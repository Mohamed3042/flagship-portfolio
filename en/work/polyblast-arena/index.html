Simulation architecture · Godot

The offline match runs like a local server.

Polyblast Arena is an original Godot 4.7 FPS with nine weapons, four maps and three modes. A fixed 60 Hz simulation owns movement, combat, bots and scoring; the client only sends intents and presents state.

The architecture call

Do not bake authority into presentation.

Even offline, the human and bots submit the same intent schema. That keeps hit detection, damage and rules in one layer and leaves a clean seam for future transport.

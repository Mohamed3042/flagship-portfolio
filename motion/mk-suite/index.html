The Pack · A buyer's film

Eighteen apps, three shelves.

MK Suite gathers the owner's software in one launcher for Windows, the browser and the phone. The film follows each membership app, the owner's desk, the signed licence, the end of a term and the proposed offer: one app, a bundle or All Access, renewing or prepaid.

Eighteen apps, three shelves.

Seven membership apps, three free cores and eight apps still in review.

Four apps for creative work.

MK Voice, Montage Pro, MK Editor and CharForge Studio, each on its real product page.

Tools that run the business.

Reclaim, Job Engine / Orbit and MK Business OS, which is featured in the Secret Office.

Free cores, and apps in review.

Three core tools stay free; eight listed apps are not for sale yet.

The owner's desk.

Listings, customers and hand-recorded invoices in a private, Arabic-first control room.

A licence names every app.

A signed licence lists the exact apps and lasts up to 35 days, reissued while the term runs.

No licence, no install.

The Windows companion installs only licensed apps, verifies every package and records it.

PC, browser and phone.

The same suite opens as a Windows companion, a browser catalog and a Home Screen web app.

When a term ends.

Renewing terms continue and prepaid ones end; premium installs stop, files and free cores stay.

One app, a bundle, or everything.

A single app, a bundle that saves more with each app, a preset, or All Access to six apps.

Rent it, or prepay the term.

Terms of 1, 3, 6 or 12 months, renewing or prepaid, with one shared 7-day trial; prices are a preview.

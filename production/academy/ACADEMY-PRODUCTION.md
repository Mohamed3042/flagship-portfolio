## Keyframe prompts

### ACA-KF01 — The Owl and the Letter

Moonlit exterior of an original candlelit stone academy on a crag; one natural owl carries one blank wax-sealed parchment letter toward the exact center. The full moon and the circular wax seal align optically but remain physically separate. Slow 40 mm push-through-cloud composition. End state points toward the iron gates. No readable address or symbols.

### ACA-KF02 — The Gates Admit

Original iron filigree gate at night as the same owl arrives; bars form an abstract owl-like negative-space emblem without a crest or logo. Warm stair-hall glow is the one luminous subject beyond the opening gate. Low 50 mm rise. Preserve the moon direction and owl identity from KF01.

### ACA-KF03 — The Moving Staircases

Grand vertical shaft of swinging stone staircases with small floating candle points. Two staircases appear to form an impossible Penrose junction, resolved by one landing passing behind the other. Highest landing reaches centered library doors. 35 mm upward crane composition; no people.

### ACA-KF04 — The Library of Grimoires

Long chained-grimoire aisle with exactly TEN blank book spines glowing candle-gold, arranged as two unmistakable rows of five. A far mirror doubles the aisle, but one centered grimoire has no reflection and hovers with its chain slack. Patient 50 mm lateral-track composition. No other glowing spines.

### ACA-KF05 — The Self-Writing Quill

Macro view of an open blank grimoire on a brass stand. One quill hovers above a fresh abstract ink stroke; its cast shadow is visibly half a beat ahead and completes the non-lexical stroke first. 85 mm macro push. No readable glyphs, letters, numbers, hands, or faces.

### ACA-KF06 — The Sorting Mirror

Tall original arched mirror in a stone chamber. Non-lexical gold ink motes approach and their reflection divides first into exactly six restrained colored-brass light threads; the room-side motes are just beginning to follow. 50 mm centered push. No face or human reflection, no house colors or crest.

### ACA-KF07 — The Six Portraits

Exactly SIX ornate aged-brass portrait frames in one corridor, three on each side, each containing a different abstract faceless painted master at work with one of six light threads. One painted candle casts real warm light onto the stone floor. 45 mm lateral-track composition; all six frames fully countable, no extra frames, no readable labels.

### ACA-KF08 — The Potion Lab

Original alchemy workshop with copper alembics and one central cauldron distilling abstract ore and blank photographs into a small glowing stone-and-brass tower. The cauldron reflection shows the finished miniature before the pour completes. 65 mm restrained 15-degree orbit composition. No people or readable imagery.

### ACA-KF09 — The Failed Cast

Only adult hands and forearms in dark umber sleeves, no face. A precise wand-point gesture fails: restrained proof-crimson sparks freeze into a visibly BROKEN circular ring while the miniature tower has dropped and cracked on the table. Near-static 58 mm frame, slight push. Failure is clear, not violent or horrific.

### ACA-KF10 — The Proven Cast

The same adult hands, sleeves, table, miniature and camera axis. A second cast sends candle-gold current through a circuit-like brass floor inlay; the miniature reassembles and rises through a now CLOSED green-gold proof ring. 40 mm pull-back-and-rise. The broken crimson state is gone; no text or UI.

### ACA-KF11 — The Hall of Standing Lights

Long ceremonial stone hall with exactly TEN floating candle clusters, arranged as two unmistakable rows of five above ten bare dark banner tables. Every flame leans toward the viewer as the doors open. 35 mm centered push. No extra candles or clusters; distant owl-post window in pre-dawn slate blue.

### ACA-KF12 — The Owl Post

Pre-dawn owl-post tower window. A restrained stream of owls carrying blank sealed scrolls flies outward; one centered owl banks toward a barred tower window. On the courtyard below, each visible owl shadow lags behind its bird, suggesting a delivery receipt. 45 mm rising composition. No readable markings.

### ACA-KF13 — The Restricted Section

Barred stone alcove of dark chained books; one hand-held lantern passes outside the bars, but the deep shelf shadows remain unnaturally pinned in place. A thin ledger edge glows faintly under one closed dark cover. Patient 50 mm lateral composition. No person visible beyond one adult hand; mysterious, never horror.

### ACA-KF14 — The Ledger of Casts

85 mm macro of a blank ruled ledger opening itself. One non-lexical wet ink line dries into an embossed aged-brass rule under a quill nib; a plain wax seal waits at the edge. The page contains no letters, names, numbers, icons, or readable symbols. One candle-gold source.

### ACA-KF15 — The Astronomy Tower

Open astronomy tower above the academy at night. A spiral stair arrives at a brass telescope; the constellation pattern overhead mirrors the arrangement of candlelit academy windows below without forming text. 30 mm crane-up composition from floor to sky. The telescope lens swings into the exact center.

### ACA-KF16 — Through the Lens

View through the centered circular brass telescope lens. Inside it is the exact night-sky geography of KF01: the same owl approaches with the same blank wax-sealed letter and the moon ring aligns behind it. Held symmetrical frame with a slow-push composition. Designed to resolve into KF01 and close the loop; no text.

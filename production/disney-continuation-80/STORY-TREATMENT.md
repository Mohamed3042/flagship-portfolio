# The Kingdom of Running Things — continuation treatment

The continuation begins at the actual closing image of the current film: the sealed book and its candle. A last ember becomes a paper firefly, descends through the book, restores a silent district, survives the kingdom's ink storm, and discovers that failed attempts are structural memory rather than waste. Beneath the page, many weak fibres braid into one load-bearing thread. The kingdom then raises a sky workshop, sends its hard-won light beyond its own volume, and finally places choice in a human hand. The ending does not close the circle; it leaves one deliberately open so the work can continue.

## VII — The Last Ember · DSN2-021–030

The sealed book yields one paper firefly. Its tiny weight opens a hidden mechanism and reveals a miniature night kingdom whose handless clock has stopped. The ember restarts only the nearest gears, making the one dark district—and the broken route toward it—impossible to ignore.

## VIII — The Missing District · DSN2-031–040

The firefly reaches the torn route, unfolds a physical paper bridge, and enters a silent workshop. It finds ink blocking the drive, rejects false replacement teeth, seats the surviving true tooth, and returns measured motion and light to the district.

## IX — The Ink Storm · DSN2-041–050

The repaired district wakes into a larger threat. Gates fold, anchor threads take tension, rain is routed into channels, and the clocktower bears the wind. One lantern route becomes a relay; the storm is divided into manageable loads instead of conquered by spectacle. The road remains after the rain.

## X — The Archive of Attempts · DSN2-051–060

The road becomes a stair into an archive of discarded routes, cracked seals, and kingdoms that almost worked. Eight failures are placed on an inspection wheel. Their weight builds a bridge and casts eight shadows into one open circle, revealing the door beneath the kingdom.

## XI — Below the Page · DSN2-061–070

Below the visible world lies its real support system: spool forests, tension chambers, counterweights, and quiet fibres. Three weak strands braid into one unbroken line. The resulting lift does not erase the mechanism; it makes the whole paper kingdom rise into dawn through the material that carries it.

## XII — The Sky Workshop · DSN2-071–080

An observatory unfolds above the city. A measured lantern constellation ascends into aligned orbits; a moon iris opens; paper planets and a cosmic loom route the braided thread toward one empty socket. The firefly becomes the messenger star, completing the sky engine and sending one precise beam outward.

## XIII — Beyond the Book · DSN2-081–090

The beam crosses a moon passage and reveals the kingdom as one volume among many. A thread escapes its cover, folds a paper courier, and travels across sleeping shelves. One shared spark wakes an entire library and opens a far door beyond the system that made it.

## XIV — The Kingdom Chooses · DSN2-091–100

Beyond the door lies an open paper horizon. A human silhouette takes one first step while many routes unfold and trial rings test their weight. A hand leaves a physical mark; the lantern constellation answers; every district moves. The road returns through the book, reaches an unfinished page, and forms an open circle rather than a full stop.

## Direction lock

Every transition is a five-second material operation: fold, hinge, rotate, slide, unwind, tension, braid, or settle. Each shot has one camera move. Candle-gold is the only warm luminous color against charcoal paper, parchment, felt, wax, wire, and tarnished brass. No dialogue, labels, faces, logos, or explanatory overlays are needed; cause and consequence remain visible in the objects themselves.

# Cake Studio — director's cut

Status: **Phase 1 / still-production gate**. No WAN credits may be spent until the 51-image contact sheet is approved.

## Film idea

The Cake Studio world is a continuous, reversible walk through an impossible patisserie. A blank edible sheet becomes a cake, a cake becomes a WebGL surface, colour patches become calibrated light, a steel rule becomes a bridge, and a proof seal binds the exact revision. The closing sheet curls back into the opening pose so the film can reverse or loop without a discontinuity.

The story is tactile, not dashboard-like. Generated pixels carry only materials and action. All product names, numbers, metrics, Arabic, English, and version text remain accessible DOM typography in the eventual page.

## Locked visual system

- World: optical-illusion patisserie built from black marble, deep-teal glass, ivory buttercream, and rose-gold metal.
- Light: one volumetric bakery backlight through fine flour haze; soft rose-gold edge light; protected black levels.
- Lens: 40 mm large-format baseline; macro lens only for the piping-tip chapter; restrained depth of field.
- Texture: photoreal buttercream pores, edible-paper fibres, polished marble veining, fine restrained film grain.
- Illusions: Escher-stacked tiers, one-view anamorphic icing, forced-perspective piping monument, frozen mirror glaze, peeling edible photographs.
- Recurring operator: only medium-brown hands; deep-teal cuff; one slim rose-gold proof ring; no face.
- Center-safe rule: every indispensable action stays inside the middle 50% of frame width.
- Exclusions: no readable text, Arabic words, Latin words, numbers, UI, logos, watermarks, labels, extra fingers, body horror, melted tools, dirty kitchen, gloomy cellar, or accidental horror.

Exact repeated style lock:

> Photoreal optical patisserie, deep teal and rose gold, black marble, flour-haze backlight, restrained film grain.

Exact repeated identity lock:

> Same operator identity: medium-brown hands, deep-teal cuff, one slim rose-gold proof ring, no visible face.

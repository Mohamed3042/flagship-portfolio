# Cake Studio continuity ledger

## Immutable locks

| Lock | Required state |
|---|---|
| Canvas | 1920×1088 PNG after non-distorting center crop |
| Palette | deep teal, rose gold, ivory, black marble; food colour only in calibration chapter |
| Key light | rear bakery window through flour haze, left-of-center |
| Fill | soft deep-teal reflection from marble |
| Operator | medium-brown hands, deep-teal cuff, one slim rose-gold ring, no face |
| Hero cake | ivory buttercream, clean cylindrical base unless a grounded product frame specifies another form |
| Proof object | rose-gold ring/seal with six facets; never readable characters |
| Critical action | middle 50% of frame width |
| Typography in pixels | forbidden; abstract cream paths only |
| Mood | premium operating bakery, never cellar, clinic, torture room, or horror |

## Spatial invariants

- Camera begins and ends 1.4 m from the same black-marble slab, 40 mm lens, eye height just above slab.
- Backlight direction never flips. Reverse scroll therefore reads as the same world, not a relit shot.
- The edible sheet remains ivory with a rose-gold edge; it is the morphing carrier across all chapters.
- Rose-gold pick marker, proof ring, and scale bridge are the same material and visual family.
- Every act bridge settles with the central subject fully visible and no active transformation at the cut.

## Gate checks

- Count: KF00 plus KF01–KF50, no gaps.
- Nine and only nine frames marked FLF.
- Exact output size for all 51.
- No text/logo/watermark rendered in pixels.
- No amputated central action under a 9:16 center crop.
- No off-model operator hands or ring.
- No visually false product count when the frame claims a discrete set (nine cakes, 20 patches, six proof facets, 12 beams).
- No WAN task exists and credit spend remains 0 before approval.
